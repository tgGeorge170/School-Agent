3rd Year — Tehničar CNC tehnologije

Official Republika Srpska curriculum, subject by subject
Years 1–2 built the foundations. 3rd year is where CNC programming and machine operation actually start.
CNC програмирање (CNC Programming)
1. Програмирање НУМА
CNC fundamentals: coordinate systems, reference points (machine/workpiece/tool zero), G-codes & M-codes, absolute vs. incremental, reading technical documentation.
2. Програмирање НУМА глодалица
CNC milling: face milling, contour milling with tool radius compensation, slots & pockets, holes, thread milling.
3. Програмирање НУМА струга
CNC turning: contour turning, grooving, drilling on the lathe, thread turning.
Практична настава (Shop / Practical Training)
1. Обрада на стругу
Manual/conventional lathe work: turning tools, longitudinal turning, tapers, grooving.
2. Обрада на глодалици и брусилици
Conventional milling & grinding: milling tools, flat surface machining, angle milling, dividing head work.
3. Припрема НУМА за рад
Real CNC operation: machine setup, control unit operation, tool/workholding/measuring prep, setting zero points, producing & inspecting parts on CNC mill and lathe.
Технологија обраде (Machining Technology theory)
Cutting theory fundamentals, sawing, turning, drilling, milling, then broaching, grinding/honing, thread & gear cutting, non-conventional machining, technological process design.
Машински елементи (Machine Elements)
Shafts, bearings, couplings, then gears (spur, bevel), worm drives, chain and belt drives.
Моделирање и симулација помоћу рачунара (CAD Modeling)
3D modeling of shafts, bearings, gears, gear trains, chain/belt drives, plus technical documentation.
Рачунари и програмирање (Computers & Programming)
General programming in C/C++ — linear and branching program structures. Not CNC-specific, more a general computing skill.
Хидраулика и пнеуматика (Hydraulics & Pneumatics)
Hydraulic system fundamentals, laws, and schematic symbols.
Термодинамика (Thermodynamics)
Gas laws, heat transfer, thermodynamic cycles, then heating/cooling basics and power plant fundamentals.
Основи предузетништва (Entrepreneurship Basics)
What entrepreneurship is, entrepreneur profile, then business plans, marketing, and finance basics.
General subjects
Math, Serbian language, foreign language (English/German/Italian/Russian), PE, plus one elective from Вјеронаука / Етика / Демократија и људска права / Култура религија.

Study Resources

Where to look, and how to use the time you have
The official curriculum doesn't name a fixed textbook — it just says "approved by the Ministry." Ask your CNC programiranje and Tehnologija obrade teachers for the exact assigned title first; that beats anything found online.

Textbooks & notes

Mašinski elementi — Miroslav Ognjanović
Standard regional textbook on shafts, bearings, couplings, gears — matches the Mašinski elementi topic list closely.
Tehnologija obrade za II mašinsku struku
Aligns with the Tehnologija obrade module.
Mašinski elementi I — skripta za usmeni
Exam-prep style notes, good for oral exam review.
Univerzitet u Banjoj Luci — Mašinski fakultet
Publishes free lecture notes (skripte) for Mehanika and related subjects — same terminology, verifiable university source.

CNC programming / G-code

CNCCookbook — G-code tutorial
Clear intro, explains mill vs. lathe programming differences.
HelmanCNC — Beginner lessons
Simple worked examples for mill and lathe, covers Fanuc/Siemens/Haas.

How to study this year

  • CNC programiranje / Praktična nastava: drill the G/M-code table until it's automatic, then hand-trace programs on paper before running them — shop time is limited and mistakes cost material.
  • Mašinski elementi / Tehnologija obrade: this builds directly on 1st–2nd year mechanics and materials — patch any shaky spots from those years now.
  • Cross-link tools to homework: use the Feeds & Speeds calculator on Tehnologija obrade problem sets, not just in the shop, so the numbers stay intuitive by exam time.
